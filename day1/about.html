About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple notes page for capturing ideas the moment they come to you. You type a note, add it to your list, and see all your notes in one place.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the "New note" box.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button.
  3. Find your note in the "Your notes" list on the Home page.

Features

  • Add a note with a single form.
  • See all your notes in one list.
  • Check how many notes you have.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field or button
Enter Add the note while the "New note" box is selected
